# 🍽️ User Engagement Analysis

## 🎯 Overview / Business Problem

This project analyzes Yelp restaurant data to understand how **user engagement influences restaurant success**.

The analysis focuses on the relationship between **reviews, tips, check-ins, ratings, and review counts**, with additional analysis of location, time trends, sentiment attributes, and Elite users.

---

## 📊 Dataset / Source

Yelp dataset covering businesses across **8 metropolitan areas in the USA and Canada**.

Five JSON datasets were used:

- 🏪 Business
- ⭐ Review
- 👤 User
- 💡 Tip
- 📍 Check-in

The data was loaded into **SQLite** for analysis. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

## 💡 Key Insights

- ⭐ Higher ratings do not always mean higher review counts.
- 💬 Reviews, tips, and check-ins are positively related to each other.
- ⭐ Restaurants around **4 stars** show the highest average engagement.
- 🗺️ **Philadelphia** has the highest calculated success score among the analyzed cities.
- 📈 Higher-rated restaurants, particularly those above **3.5 stars**, show more consistent engagement over time.
- 📅 **November–March** shows higher engagement activity.
- 👤 Elite users contribute a substantial share of total reviews despite being fewer in number.
- 🕐 The highest engagement occurs between approximately **4 PM and 1 AM**.
